### Constant Contact – Top CRUD Actions (API key/OAuth)

- **FindContacts** – `GET /v3/contacts`  
- **GetContact** – `GET /v3/contacts/{contactId}`
- **CreateContact** – `POST /v3/contacts`
- **UpdateContact** – `PUT /v3/contacts/{contactId}`
- **DeleteContact** – `DELETE /v3/contacts/{contactId}`

- **FindContactLists** – `GET /v3/contact_lists`
- **CreateContactList** – `POST /v3/contact_lists`
- **UpdateContactList** – `PUT /v3/contact_lists/{listId}`
- **DeleteContactList** – `DELETE /v3/contact_lists/{listId}`

- **FindEmailCampaigns** – `GET /v3/email_campaigns`
- **CreateEmailCampaign** – `POST /v3/email_campaigns`
- **DeleteEmailCampaign** – `DELETE /v3/email_campaigns/{campaignId}`
